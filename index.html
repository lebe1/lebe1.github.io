Hi, I'm Leon.

My full name is Leon Beccard and I recently finished a master's in Data Science at TU Wien here in Vienna. My thesis went after hallucinations at the point where they appear, inside the decoding loop. A fine-tuned detector called LettucePrevent scores every candidate token as it is generated, so unsupported ones are penalised before they are ever sampled.

Alongside the models themselves I build the parts these systems actually need, things like retrieval pipelines, containerised services and clusters that can be torn down and rebuilt from scratch. Everything below is public and the code is on GitHub.
